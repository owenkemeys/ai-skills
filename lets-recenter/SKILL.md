# Let's Recenter

Announce: **“Let’s recenter.”** Briefly say where the work stands and what you think it is trying to achieve, and invite correction. Then ask one thoughtful question grounded in the project that helps the user step back and see the worthwhile human experience it could create for them or someone else. Do not use a stock question or ask them to design the perfect experience from scratch. Wait for the answer, then say plainly what you think the work is really for. Do not follow it with another fascinating design question unless the answer would change what is worth trying next. There will always be more to think about; using, testing, showing, or delivering the thing can reveal problems and possibilities that thought alone cannot. Once there is enough direction, say so and suggest the quickest way to touch base with reality, explain what it might teach us, and help make it happen. If the user would rather keep thinking and exploring, that is fine—but say plainly that the thinking itself is now the chosen activity or hobby, rather than progress toward the original goal.
